# Balance, 2026-10-01 - every road against the Warden it expects

The owner asked for a balance pass that considers augment builds, gear, levels,
every act of every difficulty and co-op, tuned so the game is hard for everyone
and doable for the geared, the skilled and the cooperative. This is what was
measured and what was changed. The decisions and their reasoning are in
CLAUDE.md under "Every road is measured against the Warden it expects"; this
file is the numbers.

**How to read them.** `curve_report` models one wave at a time: *pressure* is
the threat a wave sends over the capability the defence has to meet it. Under
about 0.3 the wave is a formality; around 0.5 it asks for attention; near 1.0
it is the edge of what a perfect player can answer; above 1.0 it cannot be
cleared before the next arrives. The model is a **best case** - every Gold
spent the moment it arrives, every hit landed, the augment draft planned toward
its best hand - so play reads harder than this by however much a player misses.
That margin is what the bands leave room for.

Reproduce any line with:

    APPDATA=/tmp/empty LOCALAPPDATA=/tmp/empty godot --headless --path game res://tools/curve_report.tscn
    ... -- --tier=nightmare --warden=expected
    ... -- --tier=hell --warden=expected
    ... -- --tier=nightmare --warden=expected --build=towers   (or warden, town, draft)

## Before

level-1 Warden in no gear, which nobody brings there. Against the Warden each
road expects:

## Builds

Measured on the Iron Road before the tier re-tune, so the absolute numbers are
higher than the road now reads; what they show is the ordering.

| build | mean pressure |
|---|---|
| best planned hand | 0.74 |
| Warden weapons only | 0.82 |
| tower weapons only | 0.97 |
| town weapons only | 1.06 |
| drafting the game's own offers | 0.67 (after the held-card fix) |

A narrow build is harder, which is what committing to one costs; no build is a
wall once the road itself is not. The board and town builds do not grow with a
party - a party is meant to split roles.

## What the numbers do not settle

- **The expected Warden is the roads' expectation, not a measured player.** The
  level, gear and attribute split are what each road's bosses are tuned
  against. A played account on each road (`--warden=account`) is the check.
- **The Arsenal is most of the defence on the harder roads**: 55-67% of
  capability on the Long Road from Act III, 71-81% on the Chainmaker's Road.
  It grows with the Warden's Might and the board does not, so a geared Warden
  defends with their weapons more than with their towers. That matches the
  direction set on 2026-09-22 - a late game the Warden has to play rather than
  watch - but it is a design reading worth the owner's eye: on the hardest road
  the towers are about a fifth of the answer.
- **A boss's real length** wants a stopwatch on a real fight. The model lands
  every hit; if bosses still feel short, `BOSS_HEALTH_DEPTH` is the one table.

## Later the same night: the hand a player actually draws

Everything above was measured against the **planner** - a model that takes every
augment from the whole deck. A player drafts from three cards. Measuring every
build on every road (`--build=best|warden|towers|town|draft`) showed the gap,
and the model was then made to draft the way a player does: the boss draft at
its Rare floor, the Tempering as held cards to grow, two rerolls and the skipped
draft that banks one, and the first camp razed in each act (for every build).

### Before the re-tune, every build, solo (one deal each)

| road | best | Warden only | towers only | town only | drafting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Long Road | 0.37 | 0.41 | 0.48 | 0.52 | 0.57 |
| Iron Road | 0.58 | 0.64 | 0.77 | 0.84 | 0.93 |
| Chainmaker's Road | 0.66 | 0.73 | 0.88 | 0.97 | 1.10 |

The board and town builds still do not grow with a party (82-92% spread), by
design: a party is meant to split roles.

### What did not close the gap (Chainmaker's Road, the reading drafter)

| change, measured as a report-only override | mean over four deals |
|---|---|
| none | 1.07 |
| an earned evolution dealt at the next draft | 1.07 |
| four cards an offer instead of three | 1.08 |
| twelve rerolls instead of two | 1.08 |
| a purse of luck held at half its cap | 0.99 |
| the first camp of each act drafted | 1.00 |

The hand fills by Act II and then grows; what separates it from the planner is
reading the whole deck, and no deal a player can be handed closes that. So the
earned evolution and the recipe rows shipped because they are right (an earned
evolution that never arrives is a broken promise, and a recipe nobody can read
is not a build), and the roads were re-tuned against the drafted hand.

### After: the band is the drafted hand's

`hp_scale`: Iron Road 2.12 to 1.55, Chainmaker's Road 2.85 to 2.0. Damage, gear,
experience and loot untouched. The drafted hand, mean of four deals:

| road | solo | 2 | 3 | 4 | Act I | climax | planner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Long Road, expected Warden | 0.532 | 0.483 | 0.488 | 0.483 | 0.21 | 0.68 | 0.342 |
| Long Road, new account | 0.616 | | | | 0.22 | 0.84 | 0.415 |
| Iron Road | 0.628 | 0.548 | 0.552 | 0.541 | 0.25 | 0.81 | 0.384 |
| Chainmaker's Road | 0.704 | 0.601 | 0.601 | 0.586 | 0.26 | 0.91 | 0.417 |

One unlucky deal can still put the Chainmaker's Road's last acts past 1.0 (the
worst single deal read 1.10 at its peak); the mean never does. That is what a
roguelite's draft is, and the rerolls and banishes are the answer to it.

Bosses fall in 11-20 model seconds on every road, and the expected Warden
survives at least 2.4 blows of the act's own body everywhere.

### And the gear is a real requirement

`--dress=<road>` puts a Warden dressed for one road (its level, gear and
ascension, act by act) onto another. The drafted hand, mean of four deals:

| road walked | dressed for | mean | worst act |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron Road | its own | 0.628 | 0.81 |
| Iron Road | the Long Road | 0.840 | 1.09 (Act XI) |
| Chainmaker's Road | its own | 0.704 | 0.91 |
| Chainmaker's Road | the Iron Road | 0.839 | 1.10 (Act IX) |
| Chainmaker's Road | the Long Road | 1.122 | 1.45 (Act XI) |

A Warden one road under-geared gets most of the way and meets the edge in the
last acts; two roads under-geared is a wall from Act IV. That is the gear grind
doing its job - the early acts of each road drop what its last acts ask for, and
a banked front can be walked back to. Not a tuning target; recorded so the next
change to gear power is measured against it.
